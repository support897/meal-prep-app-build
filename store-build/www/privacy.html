Meal Prep

Privacy Policy

The short version: Meal Prep works fully on your device with no account at all. If you choose to make an account, your information is stored privately in your own account space so it can follow you between devices. We never sell, share or track your information.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Meal Prep is a family meal planning app made by Ilse Placencia. This policy explains exactly what happens with your information.

What we collect

Very little, and only if you choose an account. In guest mode we collect nothing at all: everything stays on your device, and Meal Prep works fully with no sign up. If you choose to create an account, the app stores the meal planning information you put in it, plus the details you sign in with, in your own private account space. That is all the app stores for you, and it is stored so your plans are kept safe and can sync between your devices.

Accounts and signing in

You can sign in with Google, with Apple, or with an email address and a password you choose. Signing in is always your choice, the app never forces it, and guest mode stays available forever. Your account lives in Google Firebase, in Sydney, Australia. Your account space is locked to your sign in, so only you can open it. Nobody else can read or change what is in your account.

What the app stores, and where

As a guest, everything you type into Meal Prep is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. That includes:

  • Family member names, likes, dislikes and allergies
  • Meal plans and recipes you add
  • Grocery items, amounts and dates
  • Calendar events you choose to import
  • Your cuisine preferences and eating times
  • Your settings, including a background photo if you set one
  • Reminder settings

If you sign in, that same information is also stored in your own account space in Firebase, so it survives if you change devices and so it can sync. Whether it sits on your device or in your account, your information is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Bringing a calendar in

If you choose to import a calendar file, Meal Prep reads the file you pick, once, on your device, and only to copy your events in. It never changes or deletes anything in your old calendar, and the file itself is never uploaded anywhere. The events then become part of your Meal Prep information, stored the same way as everything else in Meal Prep.

Photos

If you use the fridge photo feature, the photo is processed on your device by a recognition model that runs locally in the app. Photos are never uploaded, sent or stored anywhere outside your device. You always confirm suggested items before anything is saved. If you set your own background photo in Preferences, that photo is stored in the app on your device, the same as everything else in Meal Prep. If you are signed in, it is also stored in your own account space, so your background follows you. It is never shared with anyone else, and you can replace or remove it at any time in the same place.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created and shown on your device. No push notification service is used, and no notification data leaves your device.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

Meal Prep contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking of any kind. If you stay a guest, the app works fully offline and talks to no outside service. If you sign in, the app talks only to Google Firebase, and only to store and sync your own account information. Nothing about how you use the app is reported to us or to anyone else.

Sharing

We never sell, rent or pass on your information, to anyone, for any reason. There is no advertising and no partner sharing of any kind. Your account information stays in your account.

Deleting your information

Your information is only ever deleted when you delete it. The app never deletes your information on its own, and neither does anything behind it. As a guest, you can clear the app's data inside the app or by deleting the app from your device, and that removes the copy on that device permanently. If you have an account, you can delete your account and everything in it inside the app, in Settings. You can also delete your account on the web, at meal-prep-app-tau-sage.vercel.app/delete-account, the /delete-account page. Account deletion is forever: it removes your account completely, and it cannot be undone. It is the only way account information is ever deleted. You can also export your data as a file from inside the app at any time, including before you delete.

Children

Meal Prep is a general family planning tool. It never tracks anyone, including children, and it carries no advertising.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Meal Prep changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described plainly here.
